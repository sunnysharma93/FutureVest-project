## 2. Definitions

**Income Share Agreement (ISA)**: A financial agreement where a student receives funding for education in exchange for a percentage of their future income over a specified period.

**Student**: An individual seeking educational funding through the Platform.

**Investor**: An individual or entity providing funding through ISAs.

**Funding Amount**: The total amount provided to a student under an ISA.

**Income Share Percentage**: The percentage of monthly gross income that a student agrees to pay to the investor.

**Repayment Period**: The duration during which the student makes income-based payments.

**Minimum Income Threshold**: The minimum monthly income level required to trigger repayments.

**Payment Cap**: The maximum total amount a student will repay under an ISA.

**Platform**: The FutureVest website, mobile applications, and related services.

## 5. Income Share Agreements

### 5.1 ISA Formation

When you enter into an ISA through the Platform:

- The agreement is between you and the investor(s)
- FutureVest facilitates the agreement but is not a party to it
- All ISA terms must be clearly disclosed and agreed upon
- You must carefully review and understand all terms before accepting

### 5.2 ISA Terms

Each ISA must specify:

- Funding amount and disbursement schedule
- Income share percentage (5% - 15% of monthly gross income)
- Repayment period (24 - 60 months)
- Minimum income threshold (₹25,000 - ₹50,000 per month)
- Payment cap (typically 1.5x - 2.0x the funding amount)
- Conditions for payment pause or modification
- Default consequences and remedies

### 5.3 ISA Execution

ISAs are executed electronically through the Platform. By accepting an ISA, you:

- Acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to all terms
- Confirm that all information provided is accurate
- Agree to be bound by the ISA terms
- Authorize necessary payment processing

### 5.4 ISA Modifications

Any modifications to an ISA require:

- Mutual consent between you and the investor
- Written agreement to changes
- Platform approval for certain modifications
- Proper documentation of the changes
