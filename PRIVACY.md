This policy applies to:

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods

We retain information for the following periods:

#### Account Information
- **Active Accounts**: Retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Retained for 7 years after inactivity
- **Deleted Accounts**: Retained for legal requirements (7 years)

#### Transaction Data
- **Payment Records**: Retained for 7 years
- **ISA Agreements**: Retained for 10 years after completion
- **Communication Logs**: Retained for 3 years
- **Audit Logs**: Retained for 7 years

#### Personal Information
- **Basic Profile**: Retained for 7 years after account closure
- **Sensitive Data**: Retained for legal requirements
- **Analytics Data**: Retained in anonymized form indefinitely

### 6.2 Retention Criteria

Retention periods are based on:

- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Business needs and purposes
- Data minimization principles
- User preferences and requests
- Industry standards and best practices

### 6.3 Data Disposal

When data is no longer needed:

- Secure deletion methods are used
- Data is rendered unrecoverable
- Backup copies are securely destroyed
- Disposal is documented and verified
