## Getting Started

### Welcome to FutureVest

FutureVest is a revolutionary platform that helps students fund their education through Income Share Agreements (ISAs). Instead of traditional loans, you receive funding for your education and agree to pay back a percentage of your future income for a set period.

### Key Concepts

- **Income Share Agreement (ISA)**: A contract where you receive education funding in exchange for a percentage of future income
- **Investor**: Individuals or organizations that provide funding through ISAs
- **Repayment Period**: The time during which you make income-based payments
- **Minimum Income Threshold**: The salary level you must reach before repayments begin

## Account Registration

### Step 1: Create Your Account

1. **Visit the Registration Page**
   - Go to [futurevest.com/register](https://futurevest.com/register)
   - Click "Sign Up" in the top navigation

2. **Fill in Personal Information**
   ```
   Full Name: Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents
   Email: Use a personal email you check regularly
   Password: Create a strong password (min 8 characters, include uppercase, lowercase, numbers)
   Confirm Password: Re-enter your password to confirm
   ```

3. **Upload Required Documents**
   - **Resume/CV**: Current resume in PDF format (max 5MB)
   - **Aadhaar/ID Card**: Government-issued ID for verification (max 2MB)
   
   **Document Requirements:**
   - Clear, readable scans or photos
   - PDF or JPG format only
   - Files must be under size limits
   - Ensure all information is visible

4. **Complete Your Profile**
   - **Education Details**: Current institution, course, expected graduation
   - **Career Goals**: Intended career path and salary expectations
   - **Location**: Current city and preferred work locations

5. **Accept Terms and Conditions**
   - Read the Terms of Service carefully
   - Review the Privacy Policy
   - Check the box to accept terms
   - Click "Create Account"

### Verification Process

After registration, your account undergoes verification:

1. **Email Verification** (Immediate)
   - Check your email for verification link
   - Click the link to verify your email address
   - Link expires in 24 hours

2. **Document Verification** (1-3 business days)
   - Our team reviews your uploaded documents
   - You'll receive email notification when approved
   - Additional documents may be requested

3. **Profile Completion** (Required before funding)
   - Complete all profile sections
   - Add skills and experience
   - Set career preferences

## Repayment Management

### Understanding Your Repayments

#### Repayment Triggers

Repayments begin when:
1. You earn above the minimum income threshold
2. You're employed for more than 30 days
3. Your income is verified through payslips

#### Repayment Calculation

```
Monthly Repayment = Monthly Income × Income Share Percentage

Example:
Monthly Income: ₹50,000
Income Share: 10%
Monthly Repayment: ₹5,000
```

### Making Payments

#### Automatic Payments

- **Default Method**: Automatic bank transfer
- **Setup**: Link your bank account once
- **Timing**: Processed on the 1st of each month
- **Notifications**: Email reminders 3 days before

#### Manual Payments

1. Go to "Payments" section
2. Click "Make Manual Payment"
3. **Payment Options**:
   - Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS)
   - Credit/Debit card
   - UPI payment
4. Enter amount and confirm
5. Save payment receipt

#### Payment Methods

| Method | Processing Time | Fees |
|--------|------------------|------|
| Bank Transfer | 1-2 business days | Free |
| Credit/Debit Card | Instant | 2% + GST |
| UPI | Instant | Free |
| Cheque | 5-7 business days | Free |

### Payment History and Statements

#### View Payment History

1. Navigate to "Payments" → "History"
2. Filter by date range or payment type
3. Download statements in PDF format
4. Export data to Excel for records

#### Payment Details

Each payment record shows:
- Payment date and amount
- Payment method
- Transaction ID
- Status (Completed, Pending, Failed)
- Receipt download option

### Handling Payment Issues

#### Missed Payments

If you miss a payment:

1. **Automatic Retry**: System retries after 3 days
2. **Late Fees**: May apply after 7 days
3. **Communication**: Contact support if needed
4. **Payment Plan**: Options for temporary relief

#### Payment Disputes

To dispute a payment:

1. Contact support within 30 days
2. Provide payment details and reason
3. Submit supporting documents
4. Wait for resolution (5-10 business days)

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: What is FutureVest?**
A: FutureVest is a platform that connects students with investors through Income Share Agreements (ISAs), allowing students to fund their education in exchange for a percentage of future income.

**Q: How is an ISA different from a loan?**
A: Unlike traditional loans with fixed payments, ISAs have flexible payments based on your income. If you earn less, you pay less. If you earn nothing, you pay nothing during that period.

**Q: Who can use FutureVest?**
A: FutureVest is available to students pursuing higher education, recent graduates, and young professionals in India who meet our eligibility criteria.

### Investment and Funding

**Q: How much funding can I receive?**
A: Funding amounts range from ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000, depending on your education program, career prospects, and individual circumstances.

**Q: What determines my ISA terms?**
A: Terms are based on factors like your field of study, expected salary, career prospects, and market conditions. Typical income share percentages range from 5% to 15%.

**Q: Can I have multiple ISAs?**
A: Yes, you can have multiple ISAs, but total income share payments cannot exceed 20% of your monthly income.

### Repayments

**Q: When do repayments start?**
A: Repayments begin once you're employed and earning above the minimum income threshold specified in your ISA (typically ₹25,000-₹50,000 per month).

**Q: What happens if I lose my job?**
A: If your income falls below the minimum threshold, repayments pause automatically. You don't pay during periods of unemployment or low income.

**Q: Can I pay off my ISA early?**
A: Yes, you can pay off your ISA early. Some investors offer discounts for early repayment. Contact your investor for details.

### Jobs and Career

**Q: How does job matching work?**
A: Our AI system analyzes your profile, skills, and preferences to recommend suitable job opportunities from our partner companies.

**Q: Do I have to take a job through FutureVest?**
A: No, you can work anywhere. However, jobs through our platform may offer additional benefits and better alignment with your career goals.

**Q: What if I change careers?**
A: Your ISA terms remain the same regardless of career changes. The income share applies to whatever you earn.

### Technical Issues

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the instructions sent to your email.

**Q: My documents aren't uploading. What should I do?**
A: Ensure files are under size limits, in correct format (PDF/JPG), and clear. Try a different browser or contact support if issues persist.

**Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication?**
A: Go to Profile → Security → Two-Factor Authentication and follow the setup instructions.

### Privacy and Security

**Q: How is my personal information protected?**
A: We use industry-standard encryption, secure servers, and follow data protection regulations. Your information is never shared without consent.

**Q: Who can see my profile?**
A: Only verified investors and companies you apply to can see your profile. General information is anonymized in analytics.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact support to request account deletion. We'll process your request within 30 days, per data protection regulations.
